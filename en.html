Frequently asked questions

What is Nesalio?

Nesalio is a voice tutor in math for Secondary 4 and 5 students in Québec. The student asks their question out loud. Nesalio finds the concept that is really blocking them, then explains it again by drawing it step by step, following the Québec curriculum and the method taught in class. It guides without giving the answer and checks that the student can do it again alone. Parents get a progress report every week.

Who is Nesalio for?

For Secondary 4 and 5 students in Québec, in CST, TS and SN math. It is also for parents who want to help their child without knowing the subject.

How much will Nesalio cost?

The price is not set yet: we will decide it with the first families who try it. The demo is free, with no commitment.

When can I use it?

You can try it now. Click « Try Nesalio » to open the tutor directly, with no form to fill out.

Do I pay anything now?

No. The trial is free, with no card or contact details required. The « Try Nesalio » button opens the tool directly.

Is it cheating?

No. Nesalio does not do your homework for you. It explains, asks you questions and checks that you can do it again alone. On the test, you are the one who knows.

How is it different from ChatGPT?

ChatGPT and Gemini have study modes that guide without giving the answer, and that is a good thing. Nesalio goes further: it follows the Québec curriculum and the method taught in class, it draws the explanation while it talks, and it remembers what you were stuck on last week.
