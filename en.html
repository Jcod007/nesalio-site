Frequently asked questions

What is Nesalio?

Nesalio is an AI-powered voice tutor for Secondary 4 and 5 and CEGEP students in Québec. The student asks their question out loud. Nesalio finds the concept that is really blocking them, then explains it again by drawing it step by step, following the Québec curriculum and the method taught in class. It guides without giving the answer and checks that the student can do it again alone. Parents get a progress report every week.

Who is Nesalio for?

For Secondary 4 and 5 students (CST, TS and SN math, physics, chemistry, ST and STE science) and for CEGEP science students (differential and integral calculus, linear algebra, physics, chemistry). It is also for parents who want to help their child without knowing the subject.

How much will Nesalio cost?

The price is not set yet: we will decide it with the first families who try it. The 15-minute demo is free, with no commitment.

When can I use it?

Access opens gradually, starting with the first people who sign up. We will email you, or text you if you prefer, as soon as Nesalio opens.

Do I pay anything now?

No. The demo is free and we never ask for a card. Leave your email and we will write to set a time.

Is it cheating?

No. Nesalio does not do your homework for you. It explains, asks you questions and checks that you can do it again alone. On the test, you are the one who knows.

How is it different from ChatGPT?

ChatGPT and Gemini have study modes that guide without giving the answer, and that is a good thing. Nesalio goes further: it follows the Québec curriculum and the method taught in class, it draws the explanation while it talks, and it remembers what you were stuck on last week.
